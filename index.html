dock-start foiling
  • 🖐 Two fingers on the board, shove it forward along the dock — keep pushing to reach takeoff speed.
  • 📱 Once on foil, tilt the phone: nose down = faster but closer to the water, nose up = climb but bleed speed.
  • 💥 Pump — push the whole phone down — on the nose-down stroke to add energy.
  • ⚠️ Touch the water, breach the foil, stall the nose, or pump while the nose is up… splash.
Uses your phone's motion & tilt sensors — you'll be asked for permission. On a computer: ↑/↓ to tilt, Space to pump, drag to dock-start.
